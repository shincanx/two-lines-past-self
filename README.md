Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Two Lines to Your Past Self"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) — two lines to your past self: line one warns about one specific moment, line two says why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints them to stdout. No network, no dependencies, deterministic.
